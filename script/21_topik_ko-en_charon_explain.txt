Welcome to today's TOPIK Korean lesson. We are going to learn the most fundamental Korean greeting, used at all times of day. The phrase is 안녕하세요. Listen carefully one more time: 안녕하세요. The literal meaning is something like "are you at peace," but in everyday use it simply means "hello." This is the polite form, ending in -세요, which makes it appropriate for strangers, elders, and formal situations. Now let's practice in a full sentence. 안녕하세요, 만나서 반갑습니다. This translates as "Hello, nice to meet you." Repeat after me, slowly: 안녕하세요, 만나서 반갑습니다. Great. Remember, the rising intonation at the end makes the greeting sound warm and friendly.
